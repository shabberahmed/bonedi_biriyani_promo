Bonedi House · Bengali Restaurant · Miyapur, Hyderabad

Bonedi House menu — Bengali biryani & homestyle plates.

Bonedi House is a Bengali restaurant in JP Nagar, Miyapur, Hyderabad — not a clothing brand. Signature Bonedi biryani, Catla Maharani, Sunday fish platters, Bengali sweets and coolers. Open daily 12 PM–11 PM.

Signature Bengali biryani

  • Bonedi Biryani — traditional Bengali biryani, house signature.
  • Chicken Biryani — dum-cooked chicken, saffron basmati.
  • Shahi Biryani — Nawabi layered dum preparation.
  • Catla Maharani — curated signature Bengali fish plate.
  • The Royal Box — biryani with salad, chutney, sweets, Aam Panna and paan.
  • Sunday Fish Platter — Sunday Bengali homestyle fish selection.

Sides

  • Green Salad — crisp, cooling greens.
  • Green Chutney — bright house herb chutney.

Bengali sweets

  • Misti Doi — set in a traditional clay matka.
  • Rosogulla — soft, syrup-bathed classic.
  • Mitta Paan — fragrant ceremonial close.

Beverages

  • Aam Panna — raw mango cooler for spice-laden plates.
