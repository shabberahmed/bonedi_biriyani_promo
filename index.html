01 The Brand

Biryani, crafted as an art.

Bonedi House is a Bengali restaurant in JP Nagar, Miyapur — where homestyle flavour meets royal-style dining. Every grain is layered with intention. Every seal carries a promise of heritage.

From daily Bengali meals to Bonedi biryani and curated plates like Catla Maharani, we honour the slow rituals of heritage cooking — crafted with tradition & love.

02 Signature

03 The Craft

Six movements. One legacy.

01 — Rice

Rice

Long-grain basmati, aged and rinsed until the water runs clear — the quiet foundation of every royal plate.

02 — Spice

Spice

Whole spices toasted to release their oils — cardamom, clove, cinnamon, and bay leaf in measured harmony.

03 — Marination

Marination

Meat rested in yoghurt and spice until tender — patience as the first act of hospitality.

04 — Layering

Layering

Rice and meat stacked in the handi — saffron milk poured like liquid gold between each strata.

05 — Dum

Dum

Sealed and slow-cooked on gentle heat — steam trapped, aroma concentrated, legacy preserved.

06 — Serve

Serve

Opened at the table — or sealed in our royal box — a ceremony of fragrance, warmth, and appetite.
